# The playbook

How we built our four Roblox games with Claude Code: Battle for Pets (pet collecting and real-time pet battles, by two friends), Axel (racing), +1 Mecha Cyborg and +1 TikTok Fame. Each guide covers what we built, how we worked with Claude on it, the rules we follow now and why, the mistakes, short code snippets, prompts that worked, and how to start the same thing in your game.

The guides come from our notes and task logs, so "the user" in them means us, the people telling Claude what to build, and the quotes are our own words to Claude.

The guides are written at our scale, with our game's names: Clover Meadow, Seashell Coast and Starfall are areas or updates in Battle for Pets, and the Kraken is a boss. If you're new, start with each guide's "Do it in your game" section and come back to the rest when you need it.

Pick the guide by what you're about to build. Any chat can use these: tell Claude "first read ~/.claude/skills/game-coach/playbook/<guide>.md", or ask the coach with `/game-coach how <topic>`.
